# Tuition Bureau Owner & Administrator Operating Guide

Welcome to your Tuition Bureau CRM! This handbook is written in plain, non-technical language to help you and your staff manage student inquiries, verify tutors safely, find the best tutor matches, schedule demos, and protect your business data.

---

## 1. Getting Started & Logging In

### Accessing Your CRM
1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, or Firefox) on your computer, tablet, or mobile phone.
2. Visit your bureau's administrative web address (e.g., `https://crm.yourbureau.com/login`).
3. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
4. Click **Sign In**.

### Mobile-Friendly Use
Your CRM is designed to work seamlessly on mobile phones. On mobile:
- The left menu collapses into a simple menu button in the top corner.
- Call buttons and **WhatsApp** buttons allow you to dial parents or message tutors with a single tap.
- You can review and verify tutor ID documents directly from your smartphone while away from your desk.

### Logging Out & Security
If you are working on a shared computer or cybercafe:
- Click your profile icon in the top right corner and click **Logout**.
- Never share your password with tutors or parents.

## 3. Managing Students & Parents

### The Student Pipeline
Every student inquiry moves through clear stages:
- **`NEW`**: Just registered on the website or recorded by your staff. Needs an initial call.
- **`CONTACTED`**: You have spoken with the parent. You are clarifying requirements, timings, or budget.
- **`REQUIREMENT_CONFIRMED`**: The parent is ready and waiting for you to suggest a suitable tutor.
- **`TUTOR_SUGGESTED`**: You have shortlisted tutor profile(s) and shared them with the parent on WhatsApp.
- **`DEMO_SCHEDULED`**: A trial class date has been agreed upon between the parent and tutor.
- **`CONVERTED`**: The demo was successful, monthly tuition has started, and your commission/fees are confirmed!
- **`LOST`**: The parent found someone else, postponed, or had an unrealistic budget.

### Adding a Walk-In or Phone Caller (**Quick Add**)
When a parent calls your office phone or walks into your office:
1. Click the blue **+ Add Student** button at the top of the screen.
2. Enter the essential details:
   - Parent's Name & Mobile Number.
   - Student's Name, Class, and Board (CBSE, ICSE, State Board).
   - Subjects needed (e.g., Maths, Physics).
   - Residential Locality (e.g., Boring Road, Kankarbagh).
   - Monthly budget and preferred timings.
   - Lead Source (e.g., `PHONE`, `WALK_IN`, `REFERRAL`).
3. Click **Save Student**.
4. The student is immediately saved and available for matching.

### Duplicate Warnings
If a parent calls twice or re-submits a form with the same phone number and student name, the CRM will flag it with a **Duplicate Warning**. This prevents multiple staff members from calling the same parent twice.

## 4. Tutor Verification & Safety (Your Top Responsibility)

In the home tuition business, **child safety and tutor trust are everything**. Sending an unvetted stranger into a family's home can destroy your bureau's reputation. Your CRM provides a strict verification workflow.

### Tutor Verification Statuses
- **`PENDING`**: Newly registered. Not yet vetted. Cannot be matched to students under standard policy.
- **`UNDER_REVIEW`**: You have contacted the tutor and are reviewing their ID cards, qualifications, or references.
- **`VERIFIED`**: You have physically or digitally inspected their government ID (Aadhaar card) and profile photo. Approved for home visits.
- **`REJECTED`**: Does not meet your bureau's academic standards or failed initial screening.
- **`BLACKLISTED`**: Serious misconduct, unprofessional behavior, or fake documents. Banned permanently.

### How to Verify a Tutor (Side-by-Side Verification Panel)
1. Navigate to the **Tutors** page.
2. Filter by status: **PENDING**.
3. Click on the tutor's row to open their **Verification Panel**:
   - On the left: The tutor's profile details (Name, Age, Address, Qualifications, Subjects, Expected Fee).
   - On the right: The **Document Viewer**, displaying their uploaded photograph and Aadhaar Card.
4. **Carefully Check 4 Things:**
   - **Photo Check:** Is the profile photo clear, professional, and recognizably the same person on the ID card?
   - **Name Match:** Does the name typed in the application match the legal name on the Aadhaar card?
   - **Aadhaar Number & Address:** Check that the Aadhaar number entered matches the card image, and verify their residential locality.
   - **Qualifications:** Verify their degree, college, and teaching experience.
5. If everything is satisfactory, click the green **Verify Tutor** button.
6. The tutor is instantly marked as **`VERIFIED`**, stamped with your admin name and time, and will now appear in Tutor Finder searches.

> [!WARNING]
> **Automatic Verification Blocker:**
> The CRM will **refuse** to verify a tutor if their profile photo or required government ID document is missing. You cannot bypass this rule; every home tutor must have verified identification on record.

### Rejecting or Blacklisting
- If a tutor fails your screening, click **Reject** and select a reason (e.g., "Inadequate English communication" or "Under-qualified").
- If a tutor engages in misconduct, click **Blacklist** and write the exact reason. Blacklisted tutors can **never** be matched, and their phone numbers are permanently blocked from registering again.

### Keeping Tutors "Fresh"
Tutors often change their schedule or take on other jobs. When you talk to a tutor, click the **Confirmed Today** button. This resets their confirmation date. The smart matching engine prioritizes tutors who have confirmed their availability within the last 90 days.

## 5. Tutor Finder (Smart Matching Engine)

Tutor Finder is the engine of your business. It automatically evaluates every tutor in your database against a student's exact requirements.

### How to Run a Match
1. Open the student's detail page and click **Find Tutors** (or go to **Tutor Finder** in the sidebar and choose a student).
2. The system applies strict **Safety Filters**:
   - Unverified tutors are excluded by default.
   - Inactive, rejected, or blacklisted tutors are completely excluded.
   - Only tutors teaching the requested class and subject are shown.
   - Gender preferences (e.g., "Female tutor required") are strictly enforced.
3. The remaining tutors are scored from **0 to 100 points** based on:
   - **Locality (30 points):** Exact locality = 30 points; nearby neighboring locality = 15 points.
   - **Subject Coverage (25 points):** How many of the student's subjects the tutor covers.
   - **Class Level (10 points):** Experience with that specific class.
   - **Board (10 points):** Experience with CBSE, ICSE, or State Board.
   - **Fee vs Budget (10 points):** Matches tutor expectations with the parent's budget.
   - **Timing (5 points):** Morning, Afternoon, or Evening availability.
   - **Experience (5 points):** Years of tutoring experience.
   - **Freshness (5 points):** Confirmed availability in the last 90 days.

### Reading Match Reason Chips
Every recommended tutor displays color-coded chips explaining their score:
- **Green Chips (`✔`):** Perfect matches (e.g., `✔ Locality matched`, `✔ Board matched`, `✔ Within budget`).
- **Amber Chips (`◐`):** Partial matches (e.g., `◐ Nearby locality`, `◐ Budget within 15% tolerance`).
- **Red Chips (`✖`):** Mismatches (e.g., `✖ Over budget by ₹1,500`, `✖ Different timing`).

### Taking Action
- **Shortlist:** Adds the tutor to the student's inquiry file so your team can call them.
- **Compare:** Select 2 or 3 tutors to view their qualifications, fees, and travel distance side-by-side.
- **Assign:** Books the tutor for an upcoming demo class.

## 6. Managing Assignments & Demos

An **Assignment** tracks the business relationship between one student and one tutor.

### Demo Progression
1. **`SHORTLISTED`**: You have identified a promising tutor and are discussing availability.
2. **`DEMO_SCHEDULED`**: A trial class date, time, and address have been confirmed with both parties.
3. **`DEMO_CONDUCTED`**: The demo has taken place. Call the parent immediately for feedback!
4. **`ACTIVE`**: The parent approved the tutor! Tuition classes have officially commenced. Enter the agreed monthly fee and start date.
5. **`COMPLETED`**: The academic year has ended, or the student completed their exam.
6. **`CANCELLED`**: The parent or tutor discontinued classes.

### Replacing a Tutor
If a tutor leaves or a demo does not work out, **do not edit or overwrite the old assignment**.
1. Mark the existing assignment as `CANCELLED` or `DEMO_REJECTED`.
2. Click **Create Assignment** to link a new tutor.
This ensures you never lose historical records of past demos, hours taught, or fees earned.

## 7. WhatsApp Click-to-Chat & Commission Protection

Your CRM comes with built-in, pre-written WhatsApp templates for parents and tutors.

### How to Send a WhatsApp Message
1. Click the green **WhatsApp** button next to any parent or tutor.
2. A preview box appears showing the customized message with the recipient's name, subjects, and details automatically filled in.
3. Click **Open WhatsApp**.
4. WhatsApp Web (on PC) or the WhatsApp app (on mobile) opens with the message ready to send.
5. Review the text and press send in WhatsApp!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Protecting Your Bureau Commission:**
> When sharing tutor profiles or student requirements, the CRM automatically **hides parent phone numbers and full addresses from the tutor** until you officially schedule a demo. This prevents tutors from contacting parents directly behind your back and bypassing your commission.

## 8. Backups, Data Safety & Password Changes

### Downloading Your Weekly Backup
You should download a complete database backup to your office computer **every Friday evening**:
1. Go to **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Data & Safety** tab.
3. Under "Disaster Recovery", click **Backup Now**.
4. The CRM generates a single `.zip` file containing 11 clean spreadsheets representing all your students, tutors, assignments, and notes.
5. Save this file into a dedicated folder on your computer (e.g., `Documents/Tuition_Backups/2026-11-Week1.zip`).

### Google Sheets Version History (Your Second Safety Net)
Because all your CRM data lives in your Google Sheet, Google automatically saves a record of every change:
1. Open your Google Sheet in Google Drive.
2. Click **File > Version history > See version history**.
3. If an employee accidentally modifies a row in the sheet directly, you can restore previous versions with one click.

### Changing Your Admin Password
To update your administrative password:
1. Contact your system technical administrator or DevOps engineer.
2. They will run `npm run hash-password` to generate a new cryptographic password key and update the server configuration.
3. Passwords are never stored in plain text, ensuring your bureau records remain completely safe.
